How it protects your messages

End-to-end encrypted
Messages are encrypted and decrypted on your device. This server only carries data it cannot read: not the text, not the images, not the history.
Quantum-resistant
Sessions are established with PQXDH, combining the classical X25519 curve with Kyber-1024. Breaking a conversation would mean breaking both, so capturing traffic today to decrypt it with a quantum computer tomorrow gets nowhere.
Forward secrecy
Every message uses a fresh key through the Double Ratchet. Compromising one key exposes nothing earlier, and the conversation heals itself after a one-off compromise.
Encrypted history
Stored conversations are sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under a random recovery key that never leaves your devices. Without it, the archive on the server is noise.
Accounts
Passwords are stored with Argon2, never in the clear, and you can only message contacts who accepted you.
Metadata
Messages are deleted from the server as soon as the recipient has them. What the server does know is who talks to whom and when: that is inherent to having a server in the middle.
